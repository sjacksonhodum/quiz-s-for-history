### Part 1 (Definitions 1–18)

1. **USA's refusal to recognize Japanese invasion of Manchuria in China in 1931.**

* **Answer:** Soviet Union




4. **Extremely authoritarian and nationalist government controlled the control of a military dictator. (Hitler & Mussolini)**
* **Answer:** Fascism




5. **Fascist dictator who ruled Italy and was an ally of Germany during WWII.**

6. **Led by Adolf Hitler who created a racist totalitarian government who started WWII and was responsible for the Holocaust. Dissolved after WWII.**
* **Answer:** German Nazi Party




7. **Democratic President following death of FDR and makes the decision to drop atomic bombs on Japan. Served majority of FDR's last term before getting elected himself.**
* **Answer:** Harry S. Truman




8. **Secret meeting between FDR and Churchill outlining post war objectives of self-determination, no territorial expansion and free trade. US also agree the fight in Europe is the 1st priority.**
* **Answer:** Atlantic Charter




9. **Alliance of Great Britain, France, United States, and USSR/Soviet Union**
* **Answer:** Allied Powers




10. **Genocide of European Jews and other undesirables in Europe by Nazis during WWII.**
* **Answer:** Holocaust




11. **Name of alliance given to Germany, Italy, and Japan during WWII.**
* **Answer:** Axis Powers




12. **Lead US forces in Southern Pacific battles during WWII. Helps rebuild Japanese gov't and help write democratic constitution after the war.**
* **Answer:** General Douglas MacArthur




13. **FDR's foreign policy attempting to keep the world safe for democracy by providing weapons to Great Britain while promising to keep US out of war.**
* **Answer:** Arsenal of Democracy




14. **Lead and planned D-day invasion and lead all allied forces in Europe.**
* **Answer:** General Dwight Eisenhower




15. **US foreign policy to stay out of international conflicts/affairs prior to WWII.**
* **Answer:** Isolationism




16. **Agreement between the US and Great Britain for warships. The US traded ships for free 99 year rental agreement of British Islands.**
* **Answer:** Destroyers for Bases Deal




17. **Surprise attack on navy base in Hawaii by Japanese on Dec. 7th, 1941. Pushes US into WWII.**
* **Answer:** Pearl Harbor




18. **Secret military project during WWII with the goal of building atomic bomb before Germany is able to. Research based in Los Alamos, NM and Oak Ridge, TN.**
* **Answer:** Manhattan Project

### Part 2 (Definitions 19–35)

19. **A presidential decree issued by FDR in 1942 allowing Japanese American internment camps to be set up to exclude current residents believed to be a threat to security**
* **Answer:** Executive Order 9066




20. **Supreme court case allowing Japanese-Americans to be put in concentration camps due to fears of spying. Civil Liberties taken away like Schenck v. US.**
* **Answer:** Korematsu v. U.S.




21. **Japanese cities where US dropped atomic bombs leading to Japanese surrender and the end of WWII.**
* **Answer:** Hiroshima/Nagasaki




22. **May 7th, 1945 the day marking Allied victory in Europe following Germany's surrender.**
* **Answer:** V-E Day




23. **Series of laws attempting to keep US neutral if war broke out in Europe. Signed in 1935, 1936, & 1937.**
* **Answer:** Neutrality Acts




24. **Meeting of Allied powers decide on future of Germany following WWII. Results in war crimes trials (Nuremberg trials)**
* **Answer:** Potsdam Conference




25. **Massive allied invasion of European mainland.**
* **Answer:** D-Day




26. **Organization created following WWII with the goal of world peace. Still an organization today.**
* **Answer:** United Nations




27. **World leaders allowing German aggression in the hopes of preventing another world war.**
* **Answer:** Appeasement




28. **3 most powerful countries in allied powers alliance. America, Great Britain, and USSR.**
* **Answer:** Big Three




29. **State of the Union address by FDR outlining the reasons US supported the allies and democracy around the world. Believed in the following freedoms-Speech, worship, from want & from fear.**
* **Answer:** Four Freedoms Speech




30. **Law replacing Neutrality Acts of 1936 allowing US to sell goods to fighting nations as long as nations paid in cash and picked them up.**
* **Answer:** Cash and Carry




31. **Meeting of Leaders from the USA, Great Britain, and USSR meet to decide how to redraw borders in Europe following the war. Germany will be divided into 4 zones as will the capital Berlin.**
* **Answer:** Yalta Conference




32. **1st time men ages 21-35 were required to register for the draft during time of peace.**
* **Answer:** Selective Training and Service Act 1940




33. **Leader of Nazis who rebuilt German army and economy. Then started WWII and attempted to conquer the world and exterminate the Jews and all other undesirables.**
* **Answer:** Adolf Hitler




34. **Military strategy in the Pacific to get within striking distance of mainland Japan.**
* **Answer:** Island Hopping




35. **A presidential decree issued by FDR in 1942 allowing Japanese American internment camps to be set up to exclude current residents believed to be a threat to security** *(repeated definition)*
* **Answer:** Executive Order 9066
